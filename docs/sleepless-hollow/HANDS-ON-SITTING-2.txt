HANDS-ON TESTS: SITTING 2 (SCREEN SHARING AND LOGINS)
=====================================================

START: any time after 11:20 on Tuesday 3 November, or any later day.
TAKES: about 40 minutes. You need to be at the laptop the whole time.
OFFLINE: never. The laptop stays online throughout this sitting.

Two machines are involved:
  STUDIO = the Mac Studio. You see it through the Screen Sharing app.
  LAPTOP = your MacBook. This is where you type the commands.

How commands are shown in this file:
  Every command sits alone on a line between two lines of dashes. Copy only
  the line between the dashes, paste it into Terminal ON THE LAPTOP, and
  press Return. Never paste a command into the Studio.


STEP 1. GET THE LATEST SCRIPT (on the laptop)
---------------------------------------------

The script changed since sitting 1, so fetch it again. In the laptop's
Terminal, paste:

  ----------------------------------------
  scp jeremybrest@Jeremys-Mac-Studio.local:dev/keepresso-jbrest/tools/hands-on/laptop-runner.zsh ~/ && zsh ~/laptop-runner.zsh preflight
  ----------------------------------------

Expected result: the last line says  Preflight passed.
If any line says FAILED, stop and tell Claude what it printed.

There are no other files to download.


STEP 2. GET READY
-----------------

  - Plug the laptop into power.
  - Connect Screen Sharing to the Studio, logged in as yourself.
  - Have the SH Test (shtest) password ready.
  - Arrange the laptop screen so you can see both the Terminal window and
    the Screen Sharing window.
  - On the Studio, leave VS Code, Claude and Codex open. Don't ask them to do
    anything until this sitting is finished.


STEP 3. RUN SITTING 2 (on the laptop)
-------------------------------------

Paste:

  ----------------------------------------
  zsh ~/laptop-runner.zsh 2
  ----------------------------------------

How it works:
  - The script prints a countdown whenever you should just wait.
  - When it prints a yellow line starting with  >>  it wants you to do
    something. Do it in the Screen Sharing window, then click back into the
    laptop's Terminal and press Return.
  - To stop early, press Ctrl-C in the laptop's Terminal.

What it will ask you to do, in order, so nothing is a surprise:

  2.0  Nothing. A 1-minute baseline.

  2.1  Quit Screen Sharing on the laptop (click its window, press Cmd-Q).
       Wait 2 minutes (countdown). Then reconnect as yourself.

  2.2  In the Studio window, press Ctrl-Cmd-Q to lock the screen.
       Then quit Screen Sharing (Cmd-Q). Wait 2 minutes (countdown).
       Then reconnect and unlock with your password.

  2.3  In the Studio window, click the person icon at the top right and
       choose Login Window. Wait 1 minute (countdown). Then log back in as
       yourself.

  2.4  Person icon (top right) > SH Test. Type the shtest password. The first
       time, macOS shows setup screens: click through them (skip or "Set Up
       Later" wherever offered) until you see the SH Test desktop. Wait 30
       seconds. Then person icon > your name, and type your password.

  2.5  Person icon > SH Test again, then Apple menu > Log Out SH Test.
       At the login window, click your name and log in.

  2.6  On the laptop, open a SECOND Screen Sharing connection: in the
       Screen Sharing app choose Connect (or File > New), enter
       Jeremys-Mac-Studio.local, and sign in as shtest. If it offers
       "Log in as yourself", choose it. Stop when you see the SH Test desktop
       in the new window. Your own window stays open.

  2.7  Close only the SH Test window (click it, press Cmd-W). Don't log out.
       Wait 2 minutes (countdown).

  2.8  Connect as shtest again (same as 2.6), then in that window choose
       Apple menu > Log Out SH Test.

  2.9  In your own Studio window: System Settings > Users & Groups, click the
       (i) next to SH Test, click Delete User, and choose to delete the home
       folder. macOS asks for your password.

It ends with a line starting with  Done.  Then tell Claude "sitting 2 done".


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

  - Screen Sharing shows a black or grey screen after reconnecting: wait 10
    seconds, then quit and reconnect.
  - You get stuck at a login window and can't get back in: tell Claude. The
    laptop's Terminal can still reach the Studio over SSH.
  - A step doesn't match what you see: do what seems closest, press Return,
    and tell Claude afterwards which step it was. The script records the time
    of every step, so nothing is lost.
